About me

Independant art director and graphic designer based in Montreal. I specialize in branding and interactive design, but I've been working on a vast variety of projects, including prints, packaging, signage / environment design, photography art direction and more.

Over the year, I've work with a variety of brands (Doyle, Antoine.ca, Humankind, Marie-Saint-Pierre, Kombi, Kanuk, PEARLL ...) and agencies (SID LEE, Lg2 boutique, Caserne, Mill3, Cossette, Cartier, BleuBlancRouge, Okam and more)

I am always interested and looking for new projects and collaborations. I am willing to travel or relocate. Please contact me for any inquiries.
I am a dedicated and results-oriented professional with a strong background in software development, marketing, known for delivering high-quality work and driving impactful outcomes. With 3 years of experience, I have developed expertise in project management, data analysis, client relations, and consistently strive to stay ahead of industry trends. I value collaboration, continuous learning, and integrity in every aspect of my work. My approach combines strategic thinking with attention to detail, enabling me to contribute meaningfully to team goals and organizational success.

As a digital marketing student, my living standard is modest yet purpose-driven. I reside in a shared apartment near my university, which helps me manage expenses while staying connected with fellow creatives and marketers-in-the-making. My daily routine blends online classes, freelance gigs, and late-night brainstorming sessions fueled by instant coffee and ambition. I invest in a decent laptop, a stable internet connection, and a few subscriptions to marketing tools and platforms—essentials for staying ahead in the digital world. While I don’t splurge on luxuries, I prioritize experiences that enrich my learning, like attending webinars, networking events, and exploring the latest trends in consumer behavior. It’s not glamorous, but it’s a lifestyle built on hustle, curiosity, and the dream of launching campaigns that actually make an impact.
