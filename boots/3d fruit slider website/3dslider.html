About Us

Fruit juice cider is an alcoholic beverage made by fermenting fruit juice—most commonly apple—using yeast to convert natural sugars into alcohol, typically resulting in a 3–7% ABV. It can be produced from pure juice or by adding yeast, sugar, and nutrients to pasteurized juice, often creating a crisp, dry, or bubbly beverage. Non-Alcoholic Apple Cider: In the U.S., this is simply raw, pressed apple juice that hasn't been filtered to remove pulp or sediment. Brands like Martinelli’s often use "juice" and "cider" interchangeably for marketing.
