Big Hero 6

Baymax is the beloved inflatable healthcare robot companion from Disney’s Big Hero 6 (2014) and the spin-off series Baymax! (2022). Created by Tadashi Hamada, he is designed to provide medical care, featuring a soft, huggable body inspired by babies, and a personality that evolves from literal interpretations to understanding emotions.
A house model is a 3D, scaled physical or digital representation of a building, used to visualize architecture, layouts, and design features before construction. These models, ranging from simple foam to 3D-printed designs, highlight structural elements like roofs, walls, and floor plans to evaluate spatial relationships and aesthetic appeal.
